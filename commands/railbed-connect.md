name: railbed-connect
description: Check the Railbed connection, or get an approval link to connect a business in Test or Live mode.
---

Use the `connect-railbed` skill.

Call `railbed_get_connection` and tell me the business, mode and access in plain words. If nothing is connected, call `railbed_connect`, give me the approval URL and explain the Test or Live and access choices for what I am doing; I will open it myself. When I say I have approved it, check the connection again and confirm what was approved. If I asked to disconnect or switch, tell me where to do that in Railbed Developers instead of trying it yourself.
